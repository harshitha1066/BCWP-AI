# BCWP-AI
Basic commodity Wastage prediction using AI.

Predicting and alerting the villagers on the wastage of basic commodities which include 
overflow of tanks, street lights running, and electrical wastage using AI


## Problem statement-01 (Village Grid)

In many villages, water and electricity are wasted every day without anyone noticing:

- Overhead tanks overflow, wasting water and pumping power.
- Street lights keep running through daylight hours.
- Electrical loads are left on unnecessarily.

This wastage costs the community money and strains limited resources, and it usually goes unnoticed until the damage is done.

## Solution

BCWP-AI monitors village assets, detects abnormal usage patterns, predicts wastage before it happens, and alerts villagers in time to act.

## how it works
Feed information into the app or prototype
Water tanks: tank capacity, flow rate and tank model.

Street lights: the specific times to switch on and off.

Electrical wastage: bill data, to compare against actual consumption.

If internet is available: the app predicts wastage using historical data and notifies a nearby professional who has access to the app.
The professional notifies the villagers so they can act before the wastage or failure happens.
If internet is not available: previous data is stored on a memory card or chip, and the device uses it to notify the people directly.
